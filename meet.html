Biography

Abdallah is an experienced barrister with both domestic and international practice. He has appeared before all levels of court and is recognised for delivering clear, strategic counsel grounded in deep legal knowledge and practical judgment.

Over the course of his career he has acted for more than 200 clients, handled over 200 litigation cases and participated in more than 100 international arbitrations. He is also a regular legal commentator in the media.

His approach combines rigorous analysis with a client-centred focus, ensuring that every matter is handled with the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.
